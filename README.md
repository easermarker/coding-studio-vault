Tactical 2D 3v3 Volleyball Game (Working Title: Undecided)

## My Idea and Who It Is For

A tactical 2D 3v3 volleyball game inspired by *The Spike*, built in Godot for a school assignment.

The game is designed for players who enjoy tactical sports games, character roles, and RPG-style team progression. It emphasizes team composition, stat planning, and type advantages over simple stat grinding.


## What the Player Does

* **Practice in Training Mode:** Uses an open practice court to learn controls, jump timing, and hitting mechanics.

* **Inspect Team Roster:** Views their 3-player squad (Setter, Outside Hitter, Middle Blocker) to check individual stats and types.

* **Play 3v3 Matches:** Executes 2D volleyball actions including moving, serving, receiving, setting, spiking, and blocking.

* **Earn Rewards:** Wins matches to gain EXP and Gold.

* **Upgrade Player Stats:** Strategically spends rewards on attributes such as Speed, Jump Height, Spike Power, Receive, and Stamina.

* **Exploit Type Systems:** Matches Setter types with spikers for team synergy boosts and utilizes Pokémon-style type advantages (Power, Speed, Smart, Defensive) to exploit opponent weaknesses and resistances.


## My First Version and Ideas for Later

### Version 1 (Essentials)

* **Core 3v3 Volleyball Gameplay:** Complete 2D match mechanics (movement, serving, receiving, setting, spiking, blocking).

* **Basic Training Mode:** Practice court for testing controls and timing.

* **Stat Upgrade System:** EXP/Gold rewards to upgrade individual player stats.

* **Type Synergy & Advantage System:** 4 types (Power, Speed, Smart, Defensive) with Pokémon-style weaknesses, strengths, resistances, and setter synergy boosts.

* **Local Progress Saving:** Simple local save file for offline progress (no personal data collected).

### Ideas for Later (Future Updates)
